FCIS ASU Graduation Project 2026–2027

WAQI

A unified scam prevention app for Egypt. One input, one verdict, explained in plain language.

W · Web threatA · AuthenticityQ · QRI · Inspector

The problem

Egypt's response to scams is mostly awareness campaigns. They inform, but they don't protect at the moment a scam arrives. Truecaller covers only calls, desktop security apps barely work on phones.

WAQI protects: acts the moment a scam arrives, checks the message or link for you, works even if you never saw a campaign.

Five parts, one verdict

Call Protection

Voice notes and call content checked for impersonation and threats.

Message Protection

Egyptian-Arabic NLP that judges intent, not keywords. Handles dialect and code-switching.

Link & QR Sandbox

Links and QR codes opened in isolation. Follows redirects, checks domain and page behavior.

Receipt Forensics

InstaPay / Vodafone / Orange Cash screenshots checked for editing and AI fakery.

Orchestrator

XGBoost fuses all signals into one risk score. An LLM explains it in one plain sentence. It explains, never decides.

How it works

Submit anything suspicious through one app: a message, link, QR, or receipt screenshot. Auto-routing sends it to the right engines, the orchestrator fuses the signals, and every event is logged so cross-channel patterns (e.g. a number from a scam message appearing in a link later) get flagged.

Privacy: the sandbox never stores screenshots, only a plain-language explanation. Receipts: only extracted text is stored, never images.
